Teacher Workspace Notes

Of-The-Day is designed as a teacher classroom-routine tool. Teachers use it to build, save, and project classroom routines, activities, words, Do Nows, and display preferences.

What this version is for

  • Planning and projecting teacher-led classroom routines.
  • Saving teacher favorites, custom activities, routines, words, Do Nows, and display style.
  • Helping teachers start class quickly with ready-to-use classroom prompts.

What this version is not for

  • It is not a roster system.
  • It is not a gradebook.
  • It is not a student account or assignment platform.
  • It is not an AI tool.

Workspace backup

The optional workspace backup saves teacher workspace content such as routines, favorites, custom activities, words, Do Nows, and projector style.

Before paid or district use

Before a production district rollout, Of-The-Day should move to Firebase Auth and Firestore security rules, add account deletion/export controls, and publish a formal privacy policy and terms of service.

Current product principle: Of-The-Day is a teacher workspace for classroom routines.
