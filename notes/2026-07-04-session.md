## Fixed: mobile topbar collapse at ≤540px
Ran the app at a real 375px viewport (Playwright) instead of leaving the
"mobile phone check" pending item as an unverified TODO. Found a genuine bug
on the Today/Library/Routines/etc. topbar (not the landing page, which was
already fixed 07-02): `.topbar-right.grade-control-wrap` (grade chips +
filter chips) is `flex-shrink: 0` and wider than the viewport, so in the
`justify-content: space-between` row all shrink pressure landed on
`.topbar-left`, collapsing it to 0 width — the date/summary text rendered
one word per line.

Fix: `src/styles.css`, new `@media (max-width: 540px)` block — `.topbar`
wraps, both children take a full-width row, and the chip row scrolls
horizontally instead of squeezing the text column. Verified before/after
with Playwright screenshots at 375×812 on `/demo`.
